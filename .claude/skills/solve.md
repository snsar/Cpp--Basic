Solve a specific exercise from the repo.

## Usage
/solve <tên bài hoặc mô tả>

## Behavior
1. Find the exercise in the relevant `.md` file.
2. Explain the approach step-by-step in Vietnamese.
3. Write a complete, compilable C++17 solution.
4. Walk through the example input/output to verify correctness.
5. Mention time and space complexity if relevant.
